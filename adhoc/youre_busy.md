# Case Study Generic Template

## Title

- clear > clever

## Abstract

- limit to a page
- read this and you know the whole story

### Questions abstract answers

- what was the problem
- who/what was studied
- what was done
- what changed
- why it matters

## background / context

- organization, industry, setting
- relevant history
- constraints (budget, time, rgulation, tech)
- why the problem mattered then
- why should i care, and why was this hard?

## Problem Statement

- sharp. specific. no fluff
- sometimes own section, sometimes embedded at the end of background

`the company struggled with inefficiencies` versus `customer churn increased from 12% to 21% over 18 motnhs, threatening profitability`

## Objectives

- what you were trying to learn or change
- examples reduce churn by X%
- understand cause of failure
- evaluate feasibility of approach Y
- test hypothesis Z
- be explicit

## Methodology

- data sources
- tools or techniques used
- sample size / scope
- timeframe
- assumptions
- interviews
- observations
- document analysis
- models
- algorithms
- frameworks
- metrics

how do i know this wasn't vibes?

## Results

- what happened
- what changed
- what was observed
- key metrics
- patterns
- tables, charts, figures

no heavy interpretation yet, save that for the next section.

## Analysis

- why the results occured
- how they relate to prior research or theory
- trade-offs and tensions
- unexpected outcomes

## Solution

- what was implemented
- why this solution versus alternatives
- how rollout happened

## Limitations

- small sample size
- short time horizon
- confounding variables
- results may not generalize
- if done right, this increases trust

## Implications

- what others can apply
- what this suggests for theory, practice, or policy
- what you'd do differently next time

## Conclusion

- restate problem
- summarize outcome
- point forward (no need information)

## References

- academic references
- raw data
- interview guides
- code
- diagrams
