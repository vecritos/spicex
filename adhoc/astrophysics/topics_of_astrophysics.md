1. Dark Matter
   Related Quantum Mechanics Problem / Aspect:
   Nature of dark matter particles — candidates like WIMPs or axions involve quantum field theory beyond the Standard Model.
   Key Questions:
   - What is the fundamental particle (or particles) that constitute dark matter?
   - How can we directly detect or produce dark matter particles in experiments?

2. Dark Energy
   Related Quantum Mechanics Problem / Aspect:
   Quantum vacuum energy and the cosmological constant problem — why is vacuum energy density so small but nonzero?
   Key Questions:
   - What is the true nature of dark energy driving cosmic acceleration?
   - How does vacuum energy relate to the cosmological constant and quantum field theory predictions?

3. Black Holes
   Related Quantum Mechanics Problem / Aspect:
   Quantum gravity and Hawking radiation — reconciling quantum mechanics with black hole thermodynamics and information paradox.
   Key Questions:
   - How is information preserved or lost when matter falls into a black hole?
   - What is the correct theory of quantum gravity describing black hole interiors?

4. Cosmic Inflation
   Related Quantum Mechanics Problem / Aspect:
   Quantum fluctuations during inflation — origin of large-scale structure from quantum perturbations.
   Key Questions:
   - What caused the rapid inflationary expansion of the early universe?
   - How do quantum fluctuations during inflation seed the formation of galaxies?

5. Galaxy Formation and Evolution
   Related Quantum Mechanics Problem / Aspect:
   Less direct quantum connection; involves classical gravity and hydrodynamics but initial conditions depend on quantum fluctuations.
   Key Questions:
   - How do galaxies form from the initial quantum fluctuations after the Big Bang?
   - What processes govern the evolution and merging of galaxies over cosmic time?

6. Exoplanets
   Related Quantum Mechanics Problem / Aspect:
   Mostly classical physics, but quantum spectroscopy is key in analyzing exoplanet atmospheres and chemical signatures.
   Key Questions:
   - What are the atmospheric compositions of exoplanets, and do they support life?
   - How can quantum spectroscopy techniques improve detection of biosignatures remotely?

7. Neutron Stars and Pulsars
   Related Quantum Mechanics Problem / Aspect:
   Quantum many-body physics and superfluidity of nuclear matter under extreme conditions.
   Key Questions:
   - What is the state of matter inside neutron stars at supra-nuclear densities?
   - How do superfluid and superconducting phases affect neutron star dynamics?

8. Gravitational Waves
   Related Quantum Mechanics Problem / Aspect:
   Quantum aspects of gravitational wave sources and potential quantum gravity effects on wave propagation (still theoretical).
   Key Questions:
   - Can we detect quantum effects in gravitational waves from extreme astrophysical events?
   - How can gravitational wave observations inform theories of quantum gravity?

9. The Big Bang and Early Universe
   Related Quantum Mechanics Problem / Aspect:
   Quantum cosmology — understanding the quantum state of the universe and unifying quantum mechanics with general relativity.
   Key Questions:
   - What was the quantum state of the universe at the moment of the Big Bang?
   - How can quantum mechanics be unified with general relativity to describe early universe physics?

10. Astrobiology
    Related Quantum Mechanics Problem / Aspect:
    Quantum chemistry and quantum biology — understanding quantum processes in biochemical reactions and potential quantum effects in life.
    Key Questions:
    - What quantum processes are critical for the origin and functioning of life?
    - How might quantum effects influence the possibility of life on other planets?
