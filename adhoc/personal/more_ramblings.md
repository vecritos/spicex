
loneliness is a longing,
a longing to share inner self,
and a faltering of the courage needed,
finding who is buried in your soul,
and to be prepared for loneliness,
because diving into the unknown,
is when you find yourself,
but often nobody else,

when you have alone time,
that's when you face yourself,
removing external influences, turning inwards,
because not enough time here and you will never grow,
and too much time here and you will become lost internally,
you will succeed yes, but without first rooting youself,
how will you see how high your leaves have grown,

inevitably challenges will arise for you,
similar to vines and roots which twist and snake,
forging their way through all the brush you must clear,
connecting all the obsticles you will face,

to overcome them, intelligence is needed
and intelligence can be something you have,
but also something you do, it is the culmination,
of all you have learned and all you will learn, about your passions,
and when joined together with how you work, creates a path forward,
admittedly within the restrictions you do not challenge,
once the path forward is begun it is shared,
unless mythical total isolation is found,

when focusing inwards note peace of mind,
peace of mind requires finding individual significance,
without inner peace you cannot have outer peace,
a fair face fades but a beautiful soul lasts,
preserve your inner strength passionately,

find the thing(s) you believe in,
find the way(s) you connect to your beliefs,
be willing to protect them, the family, the beliefs, and yourself,
the memorable parts create the lens you see the world through, cherish them,
the best and worst moments in your life balance to make a whole you,

in your relationships communicate intent,
while leading do so openly and honestly while believing in yourself,
in the community be active and reflect when necessary,
be physically strong, metally awake, morally straight,
believe in yourself and what a good person can be,
focus on health and be patient with lasting change,

if lasting change involves a person,
and once you feel settled and stable and safe, 
and if you have a reason for wanting them in your life,
are ready to be vulnerable in life directions, emotions,
and share your personal space, but able to set healthy boundaries,
this is the foundation for a healthy relationship with another human,

avoid unrelenting anxious tongues in thought,
as they are willfully flicking inside a uncontrolled flame,
avoid hostile direct tongue fitfully flicking outside your intention,
resultant of an empty skull unfocused, unaware, holding sunken eyes,
acceptance of the path that leads to the end of suffering,
will help stay the madness within yourself,
and allow those moments to be ephemeral,

drive towards the essence of a person,
coordination among our differences can be found amidst documentation,
allow leadership and personality to shine through in assistance,
by means of a rigid yet flexible system,
