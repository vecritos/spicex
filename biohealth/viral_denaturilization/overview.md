# Filoviridae Genome Organization and Human Biological Conflicts

## Canonical Genome Layout

```text
3' → NP → VP35 → VP40 → GP → VP30 → VP24 → L → 5'
```

- Virus type:
  - Negative-sense single-stranded RNA virus
- Family:
  - Filoviridae
- Includes:
  - Ebola viruses
  - Marburg virus

---

# NP — Nucleoprotein

## Primary Function
- Encapsulates viral RNA
- Protects genome integrity
- Forms ribonucleoprotein (RNP) complex
- Supports replication and transcription

## Annotation
NP acts like a protective shell around viral RNA.

Without NP:
- viral RNA becomes unstable,
- replication efficiency collapses.

## Human Biological Conflict

### Normal Human Function
Human innate immune systems detect abnormal RNA using:
- RIG-I
- MDA5
- Pattern recognition receptors (PRRs)

These systems distinguish:
- self RNA
- foreign RNA

### Viral Conflict
NP partially hides viral RNA from immune detection.

### Result
- delayed interferon activation,
- reduced antiviral signaling,
- increased viral spread.

### DNA-Level Conflict
Indirect conflict with host DNA regulation:
- suppresses transcriptional activation of antiviral genes,
- interferes with DNA-directed immune response signaling pathways.

NP does NOT integrate into human DNA.

---

# VP35 — Polymerase Cofactor + Immune Suppressor

## Primary Function
- Assists RNA polymerase activity
- Stabilizes replication complexes
- Major interferon antagonist

## Annotation
VP35 is one of the strongest immune evasion proteins in filoviruses.

## Human Biological Conflict

### Normal Human Antiviral Pathway

```text
Foreign RNA detected
      ↓
RIG-I activation
      ↓
IRF3 / IRF7 activation
      ↓
Interferon genes transcribed
      ↓
Antiviral proteins produced
```

### Viral Conflict
VP35 blocks:
- RIG-I signaling
- MAVS activation
- IRF3 phosphorylation
- interferon-beta production

### Result
Cells fail to enter antiviral state.

### DNA-Level Conflict
Indirect suppression of host DNA transcription:
- prevents activation of interferon-stimulated genes (ISGs),
- interferes with nuclear transcription responses.

No known integration into host genome.

---

# VP40 — Matrix Protein

## Primary Function
- Viral assembly
- Membrane organization
- Virion budding and release

## Annotation
VP40 organizes formation of new virus particles.

## Human Biological Conflict

### Normal Human Functions Affected
- membrane trafficking
- vesicle transport
- cytoskeletal regulation
- exocytosis pathways

### Viral Conflict
VP40 redirects host membrane systems toward viral production.

### Result
- cellular resource depletion,
- membrane instability,
- altered signaling.

### DNA-Level Conflict
Indirect:
- changes expression patterns related to stress response pathways,
- alters signaling cascades affecting gene regulation.

No genomic insertion behavior known.

---

# GP — Glycoprotein

## Primary Function
- Host cell attachment
- Membrane fusion
- Cell entry

## Annotation
GP forms the external spikes on the virus surface.

## Entry Mechanism

```text
GP binds host cell
        ↓
Endocytosis
        ↓
NPC1 interaction
        ↓
Membrane fusion
        ↓
Genome release
```

---

## Human Biological Conflict

### Normal Human Functions Affected
- endothelial stability
- vascular permeability regulation
- immune signaling balance

### Viral Conflict
GP damages endothelial cells and increases vascular leakage.

### Result
- edema,
- hemorrhage,
- blood pressure collapse,
- systemic inflammation.

---

## Soluble GP (sGP)

### Function
- Secreted decoy protein

### Conflict
Can divert antibodies away from intact virions.

### Result
Reduced immune targeting efficiency.

### DNA-Level Conflict
Indirect:
- disrupts immune-regulated transcription pathways through inflammatory dysregulation.

No DNA integration.

---

# VP30 — Transcription Activator

## Primary Function
- Activates viral transcription
- Regulates replication/transcription balance

## Annotation
VP30 helps initiate viral mRNA synthesis.

## Human Biological Conflict

### Normal Human Function Affected
- cellular translation resource allocation
- ATP consumption
- protein synthesis balance

### Viral Conflict
Host ribosomes prioritize viral proteins.

### Result
- metabolic stress,
- reduced normal protein production,
- cellular dysfunction.

### DNA-Level Conflict
Indirect:
- shifts transcriptional priorities away from normal cellular maintenance pathways.

No known genome insertion.

---

# VP24 — Interferon Signaling Antagonist

## Primary Function
- Blocks STAT1 nuclear transport
- Suppresses interferon response

## Annotation
VP24 disables downstream antiviral signaling.

## Normal Human Pathway

```text
Interferon binds receptor
       ↓
STAT1 activated
       ↓
STAT1 enters nucleus
       ↓
Antiviral genes expressed
```

## Viral Conflict
VP24 prevents STAT1 nuclear entry.

## Result
Cells cannot activate many antiviral genes.

### DNA-Level Conflict
Direct interference with transcriptional activation:
- blocks expression of protective interferon-regulated DNA sequences.

No integration into host genome.

---

# L — RNA-dependent RNA Polymerase

## Primary Function
- Viral RNA synthesis
- Genome replication
- Viral transcription

## Annotation
The virus must carry its own polymerase because human cells cannot replicate negative-sense RNA genomes.

## Human Biological Conflict

### Normal Human Limitation
Human cells:
- do not naturally replicate negative-sense RNA,
- lack compatible polymerases.

### Viral Conflict
The L protein hijacks:
- nucleotide pools,
- ATP,
- ribosomes,
- cellular stress pathways.

### Result
- energy depletion,
- ER stress,
- apoptosis,
- inflammatory signaling.

### DNA-Level Conflict
Indirect:
- alters host transcription environment through stress-response activation.

No known DNA integration.

# Systemic Immune Response Conflicts

# Macrophages

## Normal Function
- pathogen detection
- cytokine coordination
- phagocytosis

## Viral Conflict
Macrophages become infected and hyperactivated.

## Result
- excessive inflammatory cytokines,
- viral dissemination,
- tissue damage.

---

# Dendritic Cells

## Normal Function
- antigen presentation
- T-cell activation

## Viral Conflict
Dendritic cells become dysfunctional.

## Result
- delayed adaptive immunity,
- impaired T-cell coordination.

---

## Normal Immune Goal
Inflammation should be:
- proportional,
- localized,
- temporary.

## Viral Conflict
Inflammation becomes systemic and uncontrolled.

## Major Cytokines
- TNF-α
- IL-1β
- IL-6

## Result

- shock,
- organ damage.

---

# T Cell Collapse

## Normal Function
- kill infected cells
- coordinate adaptive immunity

## Viral Conflict
Mass apoptosis occurs despite limited direct infection.

## Result
- lymphopenia,
- weakened adaptive immunity,
- immune exhaustion.

# Coagulation Dysfunction

## Normal Function
Balance between:
- clotting,
- anticoagulation,
- endothelial integrity.

## Viral Conflict
Simultaneous disruption of all systems.

## Result
- disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC),
- bleeding,
- microclots,
- ischemic injury.

# Important Clarification About Human DNA

Filoviruses:
- are RNA viruses,
- replicate in the cytoplasm,
- do NOT normally integrate into human DNA.

Unlike retroviruses:
- they do not possess reverse transcriptase integration systems.

Most "DNA conflicts" are therefore INDIRECT:
- suppression of gene transcription,
- disruption of signaling pathways,
- altered immune gene activation,
- stress-induced transcriptional dysregulation.

The primary danger is:
- immune sabotage,
- inflammatory collapse,
- vascular injury,
- organ failure.
