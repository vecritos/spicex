## original starter project

immortality of souls within a digital realm based on mappings from organic realm,
this is done via both worn armor (amor) that has been upgraded with digital technology,
as well as lifestyle efficiencies are made because more time inamor creates digital life.

three souls are created per organic user regardless of which species,
psychology of user is mapped back onto a digital generic algorithm for that species,
each individual soul exists within one of three distict worlds (specifics pending),
each world is created as one of three near-lightspeed supercomputers in space and released,
in order to live forever among the stars.

during time on Earth more automated machines are utilized with up to date technology,
current technology expectations are RFID, Bluetooth, and Magnetic-SIM enable amor interfacing,
denser bluetooth networks are reconfigured into the gardens for internet-over-bluetooth.
