# Spicex

Spicex is a living research and writing repository for speculative design, systems thinking, applied science, and practical life-building. It combines rough notes, thematic research, and a structured book project into one evolving knowledge base.

This project is not a polished product or a formal app. It is a working archive of ideas, experiments, and drafts meant to be refined over time.

## What is in this repository?

- Research and theory notes that explore systems, computation, education, and long-term design
- Draft book content organized as a markdown-based book project
- Ad hoc ideas spanning health, tools, shelter, psychology, outdoors, and other applied domains
- Experimental and exploratory documents that may become references, chapters, or be discarded later
- Supporting materials for learning, writing, and project iteration

## Repository structure

- [book](book) — the primary book project, built with mdBook
  - [book/src](book/src) — source chapters and markdown content
  - [book/src/rough](book/src/rough) — rough, draft, and exploratory notes
  - [book/book](book/book) — generated static site output
- [adhoc](adhoc) — standalone ideas and creative experiments
- [biohealth](biohealth) — notes related to health, biological systems, and bioengineering topics
- [dune](dune) — conceptual and worldbuilding materials
- [research.md](research.md) — consolidated research notes and findings
- [theory.md](theory.md) — working theory and framing documents
- [ui.sh](ui.sh) — local utility script for project convenience

## Core themes

The archive is organized around themes such as:

- education and project-based learning
- technology and human systems
- fitness, health, and physical capability
- environmental and practical design
- psychology, habit, and human behavior
- tools, equipment, shelter, and outdoor living
- speculative futures and systems-level thinking

## Working style

This repository follows a research notebook model:

- many sections are intentionally rough or unfinished
- drafts are stored in a way that allows iteration without commitment
- writing, theory, and practical notes are kept close together
- the book is treated as a living product that can evolve as the research grows

## Local book build

To build the book locally, use mdBook from the project root if it is installed:

- cd book
- mdbook build

Then open the generated site in [book/book](book/book) or serve it with:

- mdbook serve

## Notes

This repository reflects a personal learning process rather than a formal publication. Some material is intentionally experimental, and some ideas may be revised, merged, or abandoned entirely.

The goal is not perfection; it is to hold a useful, evolving body of thinking in one place.
