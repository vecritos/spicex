Mr Buckless thought it would be hilarious to attach his lawn tractor to the base of the ladder and drive away from the house at a steady rate of 4 ft per sec. "Buckless, ya trying to hill me?" shouted the betrayed, heroic calculus teacher. "Man-up, Thumser, I'm only driving 4 ft/sec. Even a pansy like you can take a fall like that!" sneered the evil stats instructor. "Fool, you should have paid attention in Calculus instead of drooling over your statistics and probability simulations. When I'm 1 foot off the ground I'll be falling at almost 100 ft/sec." Let's examine why, once again, Mr. Thumser is correct.
