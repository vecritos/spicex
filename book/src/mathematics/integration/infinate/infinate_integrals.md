# Infinate Integrals

## Infinate Limits of Integration

integrals with infinate limits of integration are considered infinate integrals, an example of this is the following

$\int_{1}^{\infty}{\frac{1}{x}\delta x}$

## Infinate Discontinuities

when evaluating integrals including an infinate discontinuity split into teh sum of 2 integrals. If either integral diverges, the entire integral diverges

### Example

$\int_{-2}^{1}{\frac{1}{x^2}\delta x}$
