# Integration by Substitution

Given the integral of the product of functions, look to see if one is a composite function. If the derivative of the inner function is a multiple of the second function, substitution is the likely method

$\int f(g(x))kg'(x)\delta x = \int kf(u)\delta u | u=g(x); \delta u=g'(x)\delta x$

**IMPORTANT REMINDER**

$\int f(x)g(x) \ne \int f(x)\delta x \int g(x)\delta x$
