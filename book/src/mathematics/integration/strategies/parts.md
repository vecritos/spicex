# Integration by Parts

$\int u\delta v = uv-\int v\delta u$

you will attempt to split the integral into 2 pieces $u$ and $\delta v$ the differentiate $u$ and antidifferentiate $dv$ so in looking for a function that becomes simpler when differentiated (or sometimes the only piece you can't integrate) when selecting $u$ and for $dv$ look for the piece that integrates easily without becoming too complicated 

> Standard Choice order for selecting $u$ becomes *(LIPET)*
> Log, Inverse Trigonometric, Polynomial, Exponential, Trigonometric
