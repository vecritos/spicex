# Integrated Integrals

$\int f_x(x,y)\delta x$ means to take the antiderivative with respect to x holding the y constant $\implies f(x,y)+c(y)$ a constant or function in terms of y

# Multiple Integrals

$\int_{a}^{b}\int_{g_1(x)}^{g_2(x)}{f(x,y)\delta y\delta x}$ means do the integral with respect to $y$ first and then do with respect to $x$
