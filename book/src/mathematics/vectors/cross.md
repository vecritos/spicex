# Cross Product Properties

$\vec{u} \times \vec{v}$ is orthogonal to $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$.

$\left\|\vec{u} \times \vec{v}\right\| = \|\vec{u}\|\|\vec{v}\|\sin\theta$

= area of parallelogram with $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ as adjacent sides.

## Triple Cross Product

$\vec{u} \cdot (\vec{v} \times \vec{w}) =
\begin{vmatrix}
u_1 & u_2 & u_3 \\
v_1 & v_2 & v_3 \\
w_1 & w_2 & w_3
\end{vmatrix}$

$\left|\vec{u} \cdot (\vec{v} \times \vec{w})\right|$

= volume of parallelepiped with $\vec{u}$, $\vec{v}$, and $\vec{w}$ as adjacent edges.

## Parametric Equation of a Line

$x = at + x_1$

$y = bt + y_1$

$z = ct + z_1$

where $\vec{v} = \langle a,b,c\rangle$ is parallel to the line and $(x_1,y_1,z_1)$ is a point on the line.

## Equation of a Plane

$a(x-x_1) + b(y-y_1) - c(z-z_1) = 0$

or

$ax + by - cz = d$

where $\vec{n} = \langle a,b,c\rangle$ is perpendicular to the plane.

## Angle Between Planes

$\cos\theta = \frac{|\vec{n}_1 \cdot \vec{n}_2|}{\|\vec{n}_1\|\|\vec{n}_2\|}$

and $\vec{n}_1 \times \vec{n}_2$ is parallel to the line of intersection of the planes.

## Distance from Point $Q$ to a Plane

$D = \left\|\operatorname{proj}_{\vec{n}}\overrightarrow{PQ}\right\|
= \frac{|\overrightarrow{PQ}\cdot\vec{n}|}{\|\vec{n}\|}$

## Distance from Point $Q$ to a Line

$D = \frac{\|\overrightarrow{PQ}\times\vec{u}\|}{\|\vec{u}\|}$

where $\vec{u}$ is a direction vector of the line.
