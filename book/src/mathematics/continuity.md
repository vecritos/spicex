# Continuity

A function $y=f(x)$ is continuous at an interior point $c$ $\iff$ $f(c)$ exists $\lim_{x \to c}(f(x))$ exists and $\lim_{x \to c}(f(x)) = f(c)$

if $f$ is not continuous at point $c$ we say that $f$ is discontinuous at point $c$ 

## Types of Discontinuities

- Removable (missing value)
- Infinate or Essential (vertical asymptotes)
- Jump (value moves from one part of the graph to another, piecewise)
- Oscillating (things like $sin(\frac{1}{x})$)
