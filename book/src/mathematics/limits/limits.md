# Limits

$\lim_{x \to c}{f(x)}=L \iff \forall\epsilon\gt0 \exists\delta\gt0 such that \lvert x-c \rvert \lt\delta \implies \lvert f(x)-L \rvert\lt\epsilon$

$\forall x$ within $\delta$ of $c$, $f(x)$ will be within $\epsilon$ of $L$

$\lim_{x \to c^-}{f(x)} = L = \lim_{x \to c^+} \iff \lim_{x \to c}{f(x)} = L$
