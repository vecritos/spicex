# Quadric Surfaces

## Ellipsoid (football, rugby)

$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} + \frac{z^2}{c^2} = 1$

## Hyperboloid of One Sheet (corset, lamp)

$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} - \frac{z^2}{c^2} = 1$

## Hyperboloid of Two Sheets (curvy cones ones upside down)

$\frac{z^2}{c^2}-\frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$

## Elliptic Cone (spinning a chopstick)

$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} - \frac{z^2}{c^2} = 0$

## Elliptic Paranoloid (superdome)

$z = \frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2}$

## Hyperbolic Paraboloid (horsey-saddle)

$z = \frac{y^2}{b^2} - \frac{x^2}{a^2}$
