# Asymptotes

## Horozontal Asymptotes

$y=b$ is a horozontal asymptote of $y=f(x)$ $\iff$ $\lim_{x \to \infty}(f(x)) = b$ and/or $\lim_{x \to -\infty}(f(x)) = b$

## Vertical Asymptotes

$x=a$ is a vertical asymptote of $y=f(x)$ $\iff$ $\lim_{x \to a^+}(f(x)) = (+/-)\infty$ and/or $\lim_{x \to a^-}(f(x)) = (+/-)\infty$
