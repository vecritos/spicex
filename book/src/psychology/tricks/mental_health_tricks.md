# Mental Health Tricks

> a revision of tricks I've used when dealing with one disorder or another

- stress disorders
  - anxiety based projects, things to correct the worry e.g. bug-out bag
  - small physiological response hacks like warm drinks and blankies
  - little corrections like taping over a cord thats on the floor
- organization disorders
  - find chaotic situations like meme feeds and then organize them into conversations
