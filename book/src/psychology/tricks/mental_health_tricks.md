# Mental Health Tricks

> a revision of tricks I've used when dealing with one disorder or another
> used as a guide in things that might help
> does not replace professional counseling

- stress disorders
  - anxiety based projects, things to correct the worry e.g. bug-out bag
  - small physiological response hacks like warm drinks and blankies
  - little corrections like taping over a cord thats on the floor
- organization disorders
  - find chaotic situations like meme feeds and then organize them into conversations

# Collecting

Collecting and collections are a visible reward center for your brain,
they deterministically show you progress that is being made,
however can trap you as well if you think you have to have the perfect collection,
rather than (quote) enjoying the thrill of the hunt for random objects that you have added value to,
many times to me it feels that depressed people will aquire lots of items to cure themselves, such as squishmallows,
the comfort is being sought, in this instance it's better to have a collection simply for the purpose of measuring progress
alongside items that make you happy, these can be squishmallows yes, or knives, or bottles, it doesn't matter as long as progress is being made,

depressive symptoms can stem from a feeling that progress doesn't exist and collections in this way attribute that for the better
