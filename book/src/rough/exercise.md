muscle group targeting

- breakdown by days you have available to workout
  - if changing schedules do so over the course of a few weeks, quick adjustments sometimes lead to lack of discipline when not externally motivated
- breakdown major muscle groups into upper body push, upper body pull, lower body, core exercises
  - do core every time you workout
  - common splits include { push, pull, lower, rest || upper, lower, rest || full body, rest }
- start small and work your way up
  - embarrassment can be mitigated by doing some exercises at home leading up to the purchase of a membership
  - many times the routine is the first thing to fail, not the body, focus on building the routine first

- upper body push
  - triceps
  - chest
- upper body pull
  - biceps
  - back
- lower body
  - legs
- core exercises
  - stomach area
