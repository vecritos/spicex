# Brainstorming

Sometimes brainstorming takes a toll on our psychological well being.
I myself have drafted some ideas that are quite out-there, and some are possibly not considerations for the dinner table.
These have included some of the following:

- Using tanker trucks with Spray Polyurethane Foam (SPF) to cushion planes without landing gear upon impact
- Adding organic-skin shielding units for spacecraft to withstand radiation on their way to Mars.
- Utilizing Ice Road Trucking chain deployment technology to give Martian rovers an extra escape plan.
- And my favorite which is having a NASA based spacecraft hunt the plaque we launched into space. 

Brainstorming is a great activity and it can lead to multiple successes if all the ideas are good.
If not brainstorming still allows for creative ideas, and discussions of different aspects of the problem.
It does not always pan out into a viable solution but it becomes worth it when we discuss the tradeoffs.
