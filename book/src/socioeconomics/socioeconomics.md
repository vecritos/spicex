# Socioeconomics

The goal of this article is not to rewrite the economy but to explore how bartering can be modernized.
We will take a look at barter and trade economies that exist further outside of pure governmentally owned currencies.
Also, we further examine how these economies can modernize the transfer of value and make things more efficient.
In addition, a small examination of how cryptocurrencies can accelerate the speed at which the economy flows.
