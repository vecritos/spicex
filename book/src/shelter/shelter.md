# Shelter

an in depth look at living simply and aquiring only that which you need to exist,
reducing one's daily footprint to allow more room for hobbies or guests or family itself

sleep sheltering should be small as can be an isolated like a sleeping pod with measurable differences in temperature,
blankets and everything should be easily sterilized and built for hygiene not comfort

the rest of many locations then becomes a hobby fueled room design instead of decor fueled design,
for example if you are a painter would you rather have an art studio or a hutch
