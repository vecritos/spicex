# Common Derivatives

- $sin(x) \to cos(x)\delta u$
- $cos(x) \to -sin(x)\delta u$
- $tan(x) \to sec^2(x)\delta u$
- $cot(x) \to -csc^2(x)\delta u$
- $sec(x) \to sec(x)tan(x)\delta u$
- $csc(x) \to -csc(x)cot(x)\delta u$
- $arcsin(u) \to 1 / \sqrt{1 - u^2}\delta u$
- $arccos(u) \to -1 / \sqrt{1 - u^2}\delta u$
- $arctan(u) \to 1 / (1 + u^2)\delta u$
- $arccot(u) \to -1 / (1 + u^2)\delta u$
- $arcsec(u) \to 1 / (\lvert u \rvert\sqrt{u^2-1})\delta u$
- $arccsc(u) \to -1 / (\lvert u \rvert\sqrt{u^2-1})\delta u$
- $e^u \to e^u\delta u$
- $a^u \to a^u\ln{a}\delta u$
- $\ln{u} \to (1 / u)\delta u$
- $\log_a{u} = 1 / (\ln(a)u) \delta u$
