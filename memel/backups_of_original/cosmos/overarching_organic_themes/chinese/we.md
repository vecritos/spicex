# We (wo men)

where are we or where is this, zhe shi na li, jer shr nah lee
we are not going, wo men bu qu, woh mern boo chwee
wo have one daughter, wo men you yi ge nu er, woh mern you ee guh nwee urr
we live in america, wo men zhu mei guo, woh mern joo may gwoh
we are married, wo men jie hun le, woh mern jeh hoon ler
we want to go to (), wo men yao qu (), woh mern yee ow chwee ()
