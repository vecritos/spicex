# Months (yue, yuu eh)

naming, yue, yuu eh
dates, ri, ree

january, yi yue, ee yuu eh
november, shi yi yue, shr ee yuu eh
this month, zhei ge yue, jay guh yuu eh
next month, xia ge yue, shee ah guh yuu eh
last month, shang ge yue, shee ah guh yuu eh
month after next, xia xia ge yue, shee ah shee ah guh yuu eh
monthly, mei ge yue, may guh yuu eh

## enumeration

add the prefix (ge, guh) to (yue, yuu)

1 month, yi ge yue, ee guh yuu eh
2 months, liang ge yue, lee ahng guh yuu eh
every month, mei ge yue, may guh yuu eh
a few months, ji ge yue, jee guh yuu eh
