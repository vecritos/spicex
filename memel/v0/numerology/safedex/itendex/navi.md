# navigation

- declination
  - find magnetic north with compass, mark angle difference on map
  - or adjust map to be in line between north arrow and top edge of map or north arrow on the map itself
- identify landmarks
- north star method
- southern cross
- constellations
  - scorpius (southern sky in summer ? ~north)
  - orion (southeast winter evening ? ~north)
  - northern crown opens towards the north
  - cassiopeia circles the north star opposite the big dipper
- using the sun
  - watch method
    - align watch with hour hand, place stick at angular position
    - adjust hour hand angle (not the time) until hour hand points at shadow line from stick
    - calculate angle halfway between the stick and the hour hand line
    - that angle points south
  - shadow-stick
    - place stick in ground, angle so it has no shadow
    - wait until a shadow of 6 inches forms
    - this is the west (stick) to east (end of shadow) line
    - south to north is listed perpendicular to this line
  - equal length
    - place three foot long stick into the ground
    - draw a circle around it
    - in the morning mark the shadows angle on the circle
    - in the afternoon mark the shadow again
    - the morning shadow corresponds to west in the east-west line
- using the moon
  - rises in the east, sets in the west
  - shadow-stick method works just as well with the moon


# Measuring Distances

- string span on a map legend comparison
- stick method (comparison to know height)
- felling method (distance on ground)
- salute method (for rivers)
- windmill stick method (midpoint calculation for width)
- compass method, calculate the shore side of a 45-45-90 triangle using compasses
