# knife

- say please and thank you when passing the knife between people
  - pass with knife closed or by holding blade
  - make sure to hold blade away from palm to prevent slicing
- verify blood circle by holding blade away from palm and outlining a hilt-circle
  - this will prevent cutting someone if nearby, never let anyone in the blood circle
- practice always cutting away from yourself
- practice cutting different materials but not yourself
  - wood (whittling)
  - foods
  - papers and cardboards
  - flowers for your mom so they can drink
  - heavily processed chemicals out of your life
- learn to clean the knife
- learn to sharpen the knife
  - learn about how different knife sharpnesses accomplish different tasks
- practice with both hands (carefully at first)
- practice with other parts of the knife
- learn about different knife points and shapes (hooks) and what they are used for
- learn about different kinds of hilts
- learn about how to whittle a { spear | animal | .. }
- practice preparing your own meal by using a knife to cut food
  - make sure you practice cleaning your knife before hand
- continue learning and take pride in your skill
- [optional] hand to hand combat only with a specialist and an obedience to safety
