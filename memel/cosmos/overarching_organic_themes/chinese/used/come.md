# Come (lai, lie)

i am coming, wo lai, woh lie
i am not coming, wo bu lai, woh boo lie
[he | she] is coming, ta lai, tah lie
[he | she] is not coming, ta bu lai, tah boo lie
i cannot come, bu neng lai, boo nerng lie
they are coming, ta men lai, tah mern lie
i will come tomorrow, wo ming tian lie, woh meeng tee an lie
