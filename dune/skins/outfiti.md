with overlain fabric weaves you can get the effect of being in just skin (with soap)
but the fabric covers you scientifically designed to get washed in in this way
usually they're a dark fabric as to absorb more light energy and stay warmer
with time the nasa space blanket materials can be sewn into the inner lining
while a more neoprene fabric is softer for the outside
these are configured to act like a skin barrier
there is a sanitation layer as well but that's detachable (open crotch) so it can be sterilized or exchanged
  this is actually the layer at which sometimes you don't recycle it
above the skin is the suit, but there's magnetically detachable layers similar to the sanitation layer
in this aspect all things are built in a layering system, but sometimes we have a coverall coverup etc (like a towel, but it attaches or is tied on)

and then from there you can just run around in skin, sanitation layer, wrap up, and have the armor off to the side
