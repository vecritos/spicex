# Counting Things (dong xi, dohng zhe)

> categories of items get special indicators

## Categories

books, ben, bern
number of occurances, ci, tsu
trees, ke, ker
buildings, suo, swoh
large & long & slender, tiao, tee ow
small & round, zhi, jr
flat things, zhang, jahng
bowls & stuff in bowls, wan, wahn
bottles & bottled things, ping, peeng
money, kuai, kwie
glasses of water, bei, bay
people, ren, wren

### Excess considerations

people or person, ren min, wren meen
[0,9] people, (chinese #) ge ren, (chinese #) guh wren
10 people, shi ge ren min, shr guh wren meen

## Examples

two bottles of beer, liang ping pi jiu, lee ahng peeng pee jeo

two hamburgers, liang ge han bao bao, lee ahng guh hahn bow bow)
