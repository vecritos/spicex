# Telephone (dian hua, dee an hwah)

- [x] local call, ben di dian hua, bern dee dee an hwah
- [x] long distance call, chang fu dian hua, chahng too dee an hwah
- [x] international call, guo ji dian hua, gwoh jee dee an hwah
- [x] public telephone, gong yong dian hua, gohng yohng dee an hwah
- [x] i want to make a phone call, wo xiang da dian hua, woh she ahng dah dee an hwah
- [x] i want to make an international call, wo yao da yi ge guo ji chang tu dian hua, woh yee ow dah ee guh gwoh jee chahng too dee an hwah
- [x] i want to call long distance, wo xiang da chang tu, woh she ahng dah chahng too
- [x] may i use your phone, wo ke yi yong ni de dian hua ma, woh ker ee yohng nee der dee an hwah mah
- [x] a collect call please, dui fang fu kuan, dway fahng foo kwahn
- [x] what is your telephone number, ni de dian hua hao ma shi, nee der dee an hwah how mah shr
- [x] my telephone number is (), wo de dian hua hao ma shi (), woh der dee an hwah how mah shr
- [x] i want to send a fax, xiang da chuan zhen, she ahng dah chwan zhern
