# feeties

> made to be worn as a wholistic unit for extended amounts of time,
> essentially worn socks but with increased durability, wicking, and thermals,
> specialized boots are worn based on the terrain underfoot e.g. skiboots, sneakers

## layers

- long liners
- recommended not to wear balls of feet pads until at least one layer worn (abrasion) 
- long wicking layer
- short gel pads layer (current configuration wrap them in duct tape to prevent rippage)
- long thermals
- short gel pads layer
- durability layer (can be cut proof fabrics currently used in gloves)
- optionally shoes of some kind

## materials

- wool
- antimicrobials
- neoprine (honorable mention)

## design

add additional velcro on top of feeties to link in with other layers in the armor
